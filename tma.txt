Here's the hypothesis about TMA.

Clauses have different functions. Some represent states, some processes, some momentary occurrences some unlikely or impossible situations.

Each of these needs a separate RI vector.

State: The sun shines, The house is yellow
Process: It rains, The ball is rolling, The water is boiling
Occurrence: The window broke, The kettle boiled over
Unreal: I would like to be rich

These three distinctive types are encoded in verb tense, adverbials, auxiliaries, clause structure, lexical choice, or transitivity.

State clauses are in present and frequently not very modified (since they typically encode background information)
Process clauses are more in the foreground, and are frequently in the progressive tense.
Occurrence clauses have typically greater information value and are the marked case: they have adverbials such as suddenly, are in simple past tense, and have other perfective markers. They are also typically highly transitive (see Halliday's extended notion of transitivity.)
Unreal clauses are, conditionals by tense or by clausal structure, often include the speaker.  

Each of these four cases of clause needs its own TMA vector. I want that vector added to EACH word in such clauses.
